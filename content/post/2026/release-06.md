The June release of Tabby CAD Suite is now available for download. This version includes under-the-hood improvements to performance and bugfixes. Some notable changes affecting customers that maintain their own custom plugins:

* The minimum supported C++ standard version has been updated to C++20.
* Advance warning: the next release will transition to the CMake build system. Please prepare your build environments and workflows accordingly.

In other YosysHQ news:

* Our CEO Nico was on the organising committee for the 3rd FPGA Developers' Forum (FDF) which was successfully held last week. Videos of all presentations are now available [here](https://indico.cern.ch/event/1549296/timetable/?view=standard).
